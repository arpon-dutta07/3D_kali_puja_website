# Sound credits

All fireworks sounds are cut from public-domain field recordings on Wikimedia Commons (no licence conditions):

- `boom-1`, `boom-2`, `boom-3`: [Fireworks piece.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fireworks_piece.ogg) by stephan, public domain
- `boom-4`, `boom-5`: [Silvester fireworks from the street 01.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Silvester_fireworks_from_the_street_01.ogg) by stephan, public domain
- `boom-6`, `crackle-1`, `crackle-2`, `fizz`: [Silvester fireworks from the street 02 cool stuff.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Silvester_fireworks_from_the_street_02_cool_stuff.ogg) by stephan, public domain

`launch` is the short whoosh from the Pixabay sound library (Pixabay Content License, free to use without attribution).

Each was trimmed, gently filtered and loudness-matched with ffmpeg.
